WEEKLY RUNDOWN

Pulls the next 7 days from Google Calendar and checks it against your regular weekly schedule (Rosary Walk, Bacon Boys, Large Group, Holy Hour, Saturday Activities), calling out anything that's NOT happening this week. Nothing is sent automatically — this just gathers the facts. Copy the result into Flocknote and write your intro around it.

Tip: on weeks Large Group is renamed (e.g. "Trivia Night"), keep the words "Large Group" somewhere in the calendar event's title too — that's how this tool tells the real Large Group apart from any other Thursday-night event.
